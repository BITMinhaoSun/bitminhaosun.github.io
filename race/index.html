RACE: Learning Multimodal Search through Referential Abstraction and Compositional Evidence

Most synthetic search questions can be solved without looking at the image or by ignoring extra targets. RACE eliminates both shortcuts—making visually grounded search strictly necessary for the answer.

01The Core Insight

Why synthetic search data fails—and how RACE locks the chain

Real multimodal search requires an unbroken chain: Image → Targets → Retrieved Evidence → Answer. Existing synthetic pipelines inadvertently leave two backdoors open, letting agents reach the right answer for the wrong reason.

02Method

How RACE synthesizes unshortcuttable search tasks

Starting from real-world imagery—social-media photos, Bilibili video frames, and MovieNet scenes—RACE builds grounded tasks, attacks them with a blind search probe to catch hidden leaks, and collects verified tool-use trajectories for one-stage SFT.

  1. 01

    Ground Real Entities

    Establish verified candidate identities (eA, eB) and provenance in natural scenes via annotations or visual analysis + retrieval.

  2. 02

    Apply RA + CE

    Replace names with visual or candidate-searched references (RA) and bind retrieved facts into a joint answer (CE).

  3. 03

    Attack with Blind Probe

    Withhold all images and run web search from the question alone: 2.4% are revised and kept, and 2.7% are discarded.

  4. 04

    Teacher Rollout → SFT

    Qwen3.7-Plus solves (I, q) with search tools; 12K answer-verified trajectories fine-tune Qwen3.5-27B in one SFT stage.

Operation 1 · Image → Target Lock

Referential Abstraction (RA)

Core rule: Never leak names in text—the solver must inspect the image (and, when needed, search candidates) to uniquely identify e*.

Mode ASpecific visual references
Appearance, spatial order, or occlusion pinpoints one person before retrieval: the third person from the left, the person partly hidden behind the post.
Mode BAmbiguous relational references
Multiple people fit the visual cue, and a retrieved fact selects the unique match: the man in the image who appeared in a crime film in year y. Construction searches every candidate, confirms one match, and rules out all others.

Formal Uniqueness Condition

M(V, ρ; F) = {e ∈ C(V, ν) : φ(e; Fe) = 1} = {e*} M(V, ρ; F) = {e ∈ C(V, ν) : φ(e; Fe) = 1} = {e*}

Unique match e* among depicted candidates C(V, ν) satisfying visible cues ν and retrieved relation φ.

Operation 2 · Evidence → Answer Lock

Compositional Evidence (CE)

Core rule: Combine retrieved facts across ≥2 targets so that all facts determine one answer, while dropping any fact leaves it undetermined.

Single ImageWithin-image composition
Two or more grounded targets in the same scene—e.g., comparing first feature-film years |tA − tB| of two actors in one still.
Multi-ImageCross-image composition (41.2% of RACE)
One grounded target per image across multiple inputs; withholding any image's contribution leaves the joint answer undetermined (collapsing probe accuracy from 40.9% → 8.2%).

Joint Sufficiency & Individual Necessity

a = c(z1, …, zk), k ≥ 2

Joint sufficiencyYc([k]) = {a}

Individual necessity|Yc([k] \ {j})| > 1 for every j

Multi-Hop Visual Chaining

Re-applying RA & CE to images discovered during search

Dependencies don't stop at the initial input. When retrieval surfaces an intermediate image J, J can directly supply visual answer evidence a = v(J) or introduce a new scene where RA selects the next search target W′:

W → retrieve → J → ρW′ → W′ → search → z(W′)

Drives sustained multi-round visual tool use (34.6% beyond round 1, 28.8% visual–web–visual chains).

Zero-Leakage Quality Gate

Blind question-only search probe & retention criterion

After static checks block direct name/answer leaks, a bounded search probe attempts the question without any images. If web search recovers a target identity, narrows candidates, or guesses the answer, feedback guides reference/relation edits:

gref(T) = gsup(T) = gdep(T) = 1, b(T) = 0

Requires reference grounding, source support, fact & image necessity, and zero shortcut bypass (b(T) = 0).

03Results

Higher accuracy on nine of ten benchmarks—from only 12K trajectories

Under strictly identical backbone (Qwen3.5-27B), tools, turn limits, decoding, and 4-run evaluation per question, RACE-27B improves all four primary multimodal search benchmarks by 2.2–5.0 points (+3.2 on average) and outperforms 28K open-source trajectories across the board.

+6.3 / +5.0 Largest gains on hard search benchmarks: BrowseComp-VL (+6.3) & BrowseComp-V³ (+5.0)
12K > 28K 12K RACE trajectories beat 28K open-source trajectories by 0.4–3.0 pts on all 4 primary benchmarks
9 / 10 Up Broad search gains while preserving factual VQA (−0.6 on MMSearch to +1.1 on FVQA; 53.5 → 56.0 overall)

Data Quality vs. Data Volume

12K shortcut-free trajectories beat 28K open-source trajectories

With the exact same Qwen3.5-27B backbone, SFT on only 12K RACE trajectories surpasses SFT on a 28K open-source mixture (combining OpenSearch-VL, Vision-DeepResearch, and REDSearcher-MM) by 0.4–3.0 percentage points on every primary benchmark.

Even the single-target RA-only (12K) variant exceeds the 28K open-source mixture on average across the four primary benchmarks (39.2 vs. 38.7), and adding CE pushes full RACE to 40.5.

04Why It Works

Six controlled tests prove the dependency drives the gain

Is RACE better simply because it has multi-image tasks or more tool calls? Controlled ablations, blind image-removal probes, trajectory audits, and test-time masking rule out superficial explanations.

With identical teacher, backbone, and 12K budget, RA alone adds +1.9 pts over the base agent, and adding CE adds another +1.3 pts across the four primary benchmarks.

Paper Summary

Abstract

Training multimodal search agents requires tasks that make visually grounded search necessary for determining the answer. Textual identity clues and unused target evidence allow shortcuts in synthetic questions, weakening supervision for visual search and evidence integration.

We propose RACE, an image-conditioned task synthesis framework that constructs dependencies from images to targets and from target-specific evidence to answers.

Read full paper abstractShow less

Referential Abstraction (RA) replaces identity-revealing text with specific visual references or ambiguous relational references resolved by searching among depicted candidates. Compositional Evidence (CE) links retrieved facts about multiple entities in one image or entities from different images into joint questions whose answers require every designated contribution. Both operations also apply to images found during search, allowing new visual observations to determine subsequent search targets or supply answer evidence.

Fine-tuning Qwen3.5-27B on only 12K teacher-generated trajectories from RACE raises average accuracy across ten multimodal benchmarks from 53.5 to 56.0, a relative improvement of approximately 4.7% over the matched base agent. Our code, data, and model will be publicly available.

Three Contributions

  1. 1

    Dependency-first formulation of task synthesis

    Unifies textual identity leakage and unused target evidence as breaks in the dependency chain from images, through targets and retrieved evidence, to answers.

  2. 2

    RACE: Referential Abstraction + Compositional Evidence

    Pairs unique image-grounded target resolution with individually necessary multi-target composition—within scenes, across images, and recursively across retrieved images.

  3. 3

    Data-efficient SFT & deeper visual search

    Only 12K teacher trajectories lift Qwen3.5-27B from 53.5 to 56.0 across ten benchmarks, beat 28K open-source trajectories, and boost multi-round visual tool use.
